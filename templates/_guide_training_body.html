Welcome, Guide,

Thank you for your love and service.

Module 1 – Your role and our promise

When someone fills out the “Curious” form on our website, they are asking to talk with a real person who has been through the training. That is you. With excitement, but no pressure or sales pitch, share how the training changed your life and answer their questions.

If they are not ready, ask permission to follow up. When they are ready, guide them through registration and payment, and stay in touch until they show up at the door. In years past one staff person did this, and the training never grew partly because it is too big a job for one person. That is why we are asking you. We are so grateful.

The “Curious” form

This is exactly what the person sees, so you know what they were told before you speak.

  • The promise: a volunteer will reach out within about two hours (8 am–8 pm Central), with no pressure. They are given your name and number so they know it is us.
  • Phone is optional. If you have no number, reach them by email.
  • Which program: they may choose “Not sure yet.” Helping them find the right fit is part of your conversation.
  • How they want to be reached is required: call or text, text only, call only, or email only. Always honor it.
  • “Anything you’d like us to know?” Read it first. It is often the best clue to what they need.

Module 2 – Get set up (one time)

  1. Give your permission. The person is told your name and cell number, so we need your OK to share your name, cell and email with Curious form applicants. Your Guide Team Captain sends you a personal link. Open it once; it takes about a minute. You can withdraw permission anytime by telling your Guide Team Captain or Kent Hafemann.
  2. Set up phone alerts with Pushover. You get an email and a phone alert when someone wants to be contacted. Pushover is free for 30 days, then a one-time $4.99 (not a subscription).
    • Search the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) for Pushover and install it.
    • Tap Sign Up, create your account, and tap the verification link Pushover emails you. Allow notifications when asked.
    • Find your User Key at the top of the app (30 letters and numbers) and send it to your Guide Team Captain, who enters it for you. Ask for a test alert. Tapping it opens this training.
    • No alerts? Check that notifications are allowed for Pushover in your phone’s Settings, that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not silencing it, and the ring/silent switch.

Module 3 – Your personal page

Every request comes with a private web page made just for you. There is no login or password. Tap the phone alert or the email button to open it, and come back anytime the same way. The link is yours alone; please do not share it. It works on a phone or a computer, but a phone is easiest because Call and Text open your own phone.

Taking a request

Tap “Yes, I’ll reach out within 2 hours.” The first Guide to tap gets it. If someone else already did, you will get the next one. If you cannot make the two-hour window, do not tap yes.

What your page looks like

Sample client shown (made up). You will see their name, cell, how they want to be contacted, the program, and what they wrote. The blue Your next step box always tells you the one thing to do now, and the checklist ticks itself off as the client moves forward.

Your part, step by step

  1. Reach out with the Call or Text button, then at the bottom choose how it went: Spoke with them, Left a voicemail, Texted, No answer, Not interested, or Wrong number. Add a short note: what they hope for, their questions, the next step. “Spoke with them,” “Not interested” and “Wrong number” close the request; the others keep it open, so update it after your next try. If you have not logged it after two hours you get a friendly reminder.
  2. When they are ready to register, use Text it from my phone or Email it to send their personal link. It already has their name, email and cell filled in; they only pick a date (a couple adds the partner). They are enrolled right away. If the date is full, a team member will help them find another.
  3. After they reserve, send the questionnaire link the same way (each partner has their own private link; they can stop and resume), then the payment request and the long form link for their contact information and a photo.
  4. If payment is late, we nudge you every 7 days. Sending a reminder is your call.

Your checklist for each client: talked with them · reserved a seat · contact information (long form) complete · photo added · questionnaire complete · payment received. You never tick these yourself.

Once payment arrives, we automatically send “Thank you – We’re so excited,” and “What to bring” one week before the training. You can read every letter on the letters we send to clients.

Module 4 – The conversation

  1. Reach out the way they asked. Start warmly: “Hi, this is [your name] with Discovery! You asked to hear more about [training]. Is now a good time?” If not, offer a time and text to confirm.
  2. Listen first. Ask what made them curious and what they are hoping for.
  3. Share your story, briefly and honestly: what you were facing, what you experienced, what changed.
  4. Answer honestly. If you do not know, say “I’ll find out.” Do not guess about prices, dates or promises. Questions you cannot answer go to your Guide Team Captain.

If you cannot reach them, leave a short friendly voicemail or text and try again that day or the next, and choose the matching outcome so the request stays open.

Module 5 – Respect and privacy

  • Only reach out to people who asked, and only the way they asked.
  • If someone says they are not interested, thank them, log it, and do not follow up again.
  • Keep what people tell you private. Do not share their name, number or story outside the team.

Questions or stuck? Contact your Guide Team Captain. Their name and number are at the bottom of your personal page.
