What it is. The Profile (also called the long form) is the “Complete your profile” page, which gives us the full picture of a person. It opens from the person's own private link, saves as they type so nothing is lost, and Submit is their “I'm done.”

What it asks. Contact details and a photo, address, partner, emergency contact, how they heard about us, their sponsor, background, health, food and access needs, and their Discovery! history.

Discovery! history. The form asks “Have you attended a Discovery! training?” If they say Yes, it asks for the month and year of each training they took: D1, D2, D3, Refocus and Relationship Training. People who say Yes are also asked to review their contract information.

What it does. It fills in the person's record in Contacts. When they submit, they receive the profile thank-you, which offers a way to ask us to reach out and the Preliminary Business Plan. Past trainees are also invited to share their experience.

How it ties to the short form. The short form creates the person's record, and the profile adds to that same record through a private link. The link is emailed automatically to people who tick Volunteer on the Discovery! short form. For anyone else, a team member can copy the “Complete-your-profile link” from the person's contact record and send it. The Guide uses the profile to make sure the contact information, including a photo, is complete before the person arrives.
