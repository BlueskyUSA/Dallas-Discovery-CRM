The Excitement Is Building!!!

Calling all you amazing Facilitators, TA's and many other Volunteers

In 2020 when the Dallas Discovery program closed (due to Covid), many of us who gave their time and heart to the training expressed a desire to re-open the program. We are in the very early stages of putting all this together, and nothing is official yet, but we are working very hard to make this happen.

This said, we will be meeting very soon with all interested volunteers. If you were a Dallas Discovery Facilitator, TA, Trainee, Admin Assistant, Sound Tech, Room/Equipment Manager, Saturday contract volunteer, or you are simply interested to know more about the training, we would love to hear from you. Please add your name and other information below so that you are among the first to know when a meeting date is set, or additional information is available.

With your permission, after you complete the short contact form, we would like to send you a request for additional information that we would use to help set up our volunteer teams. If interested, please check the box below called “Volunteer” and we will email you a request for additional information.

Thank you. Your giving hearts are welcomed, honored, and appreciated.
