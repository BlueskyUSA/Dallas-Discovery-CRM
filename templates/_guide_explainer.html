How to change a Guide

Change who is on the Guide team: open Manage the Guides to add a new Guide or remove one. Only Guides on the list receive new call requests.

Hand one person to a different Guide: open Call requests. On any request a Guide has claimed, pick another Guide from the “Hand to…” list and click Hand over. The new Guide is emailed their own link and takes over from there. The person has already been told the first Guide's name, so you may want to let them know about the change.
