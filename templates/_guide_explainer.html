How to change a Guide

Who can do this: only the Owner and the Leadership accounts the Owner has chosen (Site Access > Guides). Everyone else can see who the Guides are but cannot change them.

{% if has_guides_access %}

Change who is on the Guide team: open Manage the Guides to add a new Guide or remove one. Only Guides on the list receive new call requests, and each Guide belongs to one program group.

Hand one person to a different Guide: open Call requests. On any request a Guide has claimed, pick another Guide from the “Hand to…” list and click Hand over. The new Guide is emailed their own link and takes over from there. The person has already been told the first Guide's name, so you may want to let them know about the change.

{% else %}

You don't have this permission. To change the Guide team, or hand a person to a different Guide, ask the Owner.
