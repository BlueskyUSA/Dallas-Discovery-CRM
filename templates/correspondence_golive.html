How to turn sending on or off

Nothing on this page can be clicked to turn sending on. Only the person who can log in to Render (the Owner) can change a switch. This page just shows where each one stands.

  1. Log in to Render and open this website’s service.
  2. Click Environment in the left-hand list.
  3. Find the switch by name (for example CALL_ALERTS_LIVE). To turn it ON, set its value to 1. To turn it OFF, change the value to 0 or delete the line. Any value other than 1 means OFF.
  4. Click Save changes. Render restarts the site by itself, which takes a few minutes.
  5. Come back to this page and refresh. The switch should now show ON (or OFF).

What each switch does.

  • SESSION_LETTERS_LIVE is the master switch for letters to trainees and the team. It must be ON for the Automatic letters switch to work.
  • AUTO_LETTERS_LIVE controls the letters that go out by themselves (confirmation, “Thank you – We’re so excited”, What to bring). It needs the master switch ON too.
  • CALL_ALERTS_LIVE controls the Guide alerts, the Guide page emails, the 7-day payment reminders to Guides, and the reply to “I’m curious”.
  • BLASTS_LIVE controls Marketing & Email Blasts.

Good to know.

  • Even with a switch ON, letters go out only between 8 am and 8 pm Central. Anything outside those hours waits.
  • A switch that is OFF holds that kind of letter back. You can still preview any letter and send a test copy to yourself.
  • To stop everything quickly, turn SESSION_LETTERS_LIVE, CALL_ALERTS_LIVE and BLASTS_LIVE OFF.
  • The Email Guide Training button on the Guides page is a deliberate send to one Guide and does not depend on these switches.
  • Only the Owner and Leadership logins can see this page. Anyone with your Render login can change the switches, so keep that login private.
