Welcome to the Discovery ERP Program

This site is the working home of Discovery: a master database of trainees and leadership history, session scheduling, and the public registration pages. What you see depends on your login.

  • Volunteer Team — administer the details of upcoming sessions, such as rosters, staffing, and day-to-day organization.
  • Accounting & Legal — financial records, contracts, and reports, for the accountant, bookkeeper, and attorney. The Owner decides which areas each person can open.
  • Leadership — everything in the program, including scheduling, programs, contact lists, and the reports that show Discovery's operational history.
  • Owner — everything Leadership can do, plus transferring ownership of the program and deciding who receives Accounting & Legal logins.

The programs on this site are D1, D2, D3, Relationship, Refocus, and Spiritual. New programs, such as a training on managing your finances, can be added at any time.

Trainee and leadership information here is confidential. Please use it only for Discovery.
