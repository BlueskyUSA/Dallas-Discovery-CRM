Welcome to the CEP team,

Thank you for your love and service.

Module 1 – Your role and our promise

If within the Dallas Discovery website, a person completes (what we call the “Curious” form), they are asking for a conversation with a real person who has been through the training. That is you. With excitement, but with no pressure or sales pitch, we ask that you simply share how the training changed your life and answer any questions they may have.

If they are not ready to register, ask for permission to follow up with them. When they are ready to sign up, then guide them through registration and payment process. Our request is that you remain in touch with them through the entire process, including payment. This is a big job, and a big ask, and we are so grateful for your love, commitment, expertise, and energy.

The “Curious” form

Please look closely at this form. It is exactly what the person sees on our website, so you know what they were told before you ever speak.

  • What they were promised: an experienced Discovery volunteer will reach out within about two hours, by phone or text (their choice), with no pressure.
  • First name and email are required. Last name and cell phone are optional, so you may not always have a phone number. If you don’t, reach them by email.
  • “Which program would you like to know more about?” They can choose D1, Relationship Training, or “Not sure yet.” If they aren’t sure, helping them find the right fit is part of your conversation.
  • “Do we have permission to text or call you?” This is required. Their answer tells you how they want to be reached. Always honor it:
    • Yes, call or text is fine — use whichever you prefer.
    • Yes, but text only — do not call.
    • Yes, but call only — do not text.
    • Yes, but please email me — reach out by email only.
  • “Anything you’d like us to know?” Read it before you reach out. It is often the best clue to what they need.
  • They are told we call between 8 am and 8 pm Central, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply STOP to stop texts.
  • Someone reaches out within about two hours, between 8 am and 8 pm Central. Requests that come in overnight go out at 8 am.
  • They choose how: phone call, text message, or either. Their choice is shown on your page. Please honor it.
  • For their security, they were told your name and phone number, so they will recognize it is us.

Module 2 – Getting and claiming a request

You will know what the alert looks like and how to take a request.

First, give your permission (one time, about a minute)

When you take a request, the person is told your name and cell phone number, so they know it is really us. Before you take your first request, your CEP Team Captain sends you a link. Open it, read the short statement, tick the box, type your full name, and tap I agree. It gives Dallas Discovery permission to share your name, cell phone number and email with the people you speak with and with the team. You can withdraw it at any time by telling your Captain.

Next, set up phone alerts (one time, about 5 minutes)

Besides the email, you will get a notification on your phone, with a sound, the moment someone asks to hear from us, so you can be first to say yes. It uses an app called Pushover. Nothing is texted, so there are no text-message charges. Pushover is free for the first 30 days, then it is a one-time purchase of $4.99 plus tax (not a subscription).

  1. Download Pushover.
    • On an iPhone, open the App Store and search for Pushover Notifications.
    • On an Android phone, open the Google Play Store and search for Pushover.
    • The app icon looks like this:
    • Install the app.
  2. Create your free account. Open the app, tap Sign Up, and enter your email and a password.
  3. Verify your email address. Pushover sends you an email. Open it and tap the link inside to verify your address. Alerts will not arrive until this is done.
  4. Allow notifications when the app asks. Without this you will not hear the alert. (If you tapped “Don’t Allow” by mistake, see the steps below.)
  5. Find your User Key. It is shown at the top of the app: a code of 30 letters and numbers. Send it to your CEP Team Captain (a text or email is fine). Your Captain enters it for you.
  6. Try it. Ask your Captain to send you a test alert. You should hear it and see it within a few seconds. Tap it: it opens the page where you take a request.
  7. If you ever stop getting alerts, check that your phone is allowed to show them:
    • iPhone: open the Settings app (the gray gear). Tap Notifications, then scroll down the list and tap Pushover. Turn on Allow Notifications. Under “Alerts,” check Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners, and turn on Sounds.
    • Android: open Settings, tap Apps, tap Pushover, tap Notifications, and turn them on.
    • Focus or Do Not Disturb: if your phone is in a Focus mode (the moon icon on an iPhone), alerts may be silenced. Either turn it off during the hours you are on call, or add Pushover to the apps allowed to notify you in that Focus.
    • Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your phone.
    The email still goes out every time, so you will not miss a request.

When a request comes in

  1. You get a phone alert and an email saying someone wants to hear from us. Tap the alert, or the button in the email.
  2. Tap “Yes, I’ll reach out within 2 hours.” The first person to tap gets the request, and everyone else is told it is taken. You then see their name and cell number.
  3. If you cannot make the two-hour window, do not tap. Let your CEP Team Captain know if no one else has taken it.

Module 3 – Making the connection

You will start a warm, unhurried conversation.

  1. Reach out the way they asked, using the Call or Text button on your page.
  2. Start warmly. For example: “Hi, this is [your name] with Dallas Discovery. You asked to hear more about [training]. Is now a good time?” If it isn’t, offer a time and text to confirm it.
  3. Listen first. Ask what made them curious and what they are hoping for.
  4. Share your own story, briefly and honestly — what you were facing, what you experienced, and what changed.

If you cannot reach them: leave a short, friendly voicemail or text, and try again later that day or the next. Choose “Left a voicemail,” “Texted – waiting for a reply” or “No answer – will try again” to keep the request open.

Module 4 – Answering questions

You will answer honestly and know when to say “I’ll find out.”

  • Answer from your own experience of the training.
  • If you do not know, say so and tell them you will find out. Do not guess about prices, dates or promises.
  • Questions you cannot answer go to your CEP Team Captain.

Module 5 – Helping them register and sending the questionnaire

You will send a personal registration link in under a minute.

  1. When they are ready, find the “Ready to register?” box on your page.
  2. “Text it from my phone” opens a text to them with their personal link already written. “Email it” sends the same link by email.
  3. The link already has their name, email and cell filled in. They choose a date. For the Relationship Training they also add their partner.
  4. They are enrolled right away, and payment comes later. We email them the details.
  5. Once they have reserved a date, a “Send the questionnaire” box appears on your page. Text their private link from your phone, or tap “Email it.” In a couple, each partner has their own link, so emailing sends each person theirs. The questionnaire can be closed and reopened from the same link, so they can take their time. The Relationship Training and D1 have different questionnaires.
  6. If their date is full, they are told a team member will reach out to find another date, and we are notified.

Module 6 – Logging the outcome and following up

You will leave the next person everything they need to know.

  • On your page, choose how it went and add a short note: what they hope for and any next step.
  • “Spoke with them” closes the request. “Wrong number” and “Not interested” also close it.
  • If you claimed a request and have not logged it after two hours, you will get a friendly reminder.

Module 7 – Respect and privacy

You will protect the trust people place in us.

  • Only reach out to people who asked, and only the way they asked.
  • If someone says they are not interested, thank them, log it, and do not follow up again.
  • Keep what people tell you private. Do not share their name, number or story outside the team.

Questions or stuck? Contact your CEP Team Captain. Their name and number are at the bottom of your call page.
