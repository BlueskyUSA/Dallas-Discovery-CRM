Module 1 – Welcome to the CEP team

Introduction: Your Role and Our Promise

In the Dallas Discovery website, when a person completes (what we call the “Curious” Form), they are asking for a conversation with a real person who has been through the training. That is you. With excitement, but with no pressure or sales pitch, we ask that you simply share how the training changed your life and answer any questions they may have.

If they are not ready to register, ask for permission to follow up with them. When they are ready to sign up, then guide them through the registration and payment processes using the easy-to-use “CEP Web Page” (discussed below).

Welcome

Your role is very important. In years past, and for good reasons, only a few Dallas Discovery staff members handled most of the hard work required to fill a Discovery program with trainees. Fortunately, today we live in a different world, and we believe AI can help automate most of the tasks required to make Dallas Discovery successful. As a CEP Team member, with the help of AI, we are asking that you steward the processes discussed below to fill the training seats. This is a big job and a big ask.

Initially, we believe at least twelve people will be needed for this activity, and of course, we will add qualified team members or take away from the CEP team as may be needed. To kick things off, we plan to send an email blast to the entire Dallas Discovery database with information on how to get help to register family and friends. Many of these folks might reach out to the CEP team. The timing and details about this will be discussed during our first CEP team meeting. In addition, we are planning a special marketing effort to capture folks who are new to Discovery. So we hope you will be quite busy asking us to bring on more CEP team members.

Thank you for your willingness to serve on the Dallas Discovery CEP team. Please keep the CEP Team Captain informed on how you are doing, and send suggestions for improvements that should be made to the Dallas Discovery portal.

Module 2 – The “Curious” Form

Please look closely at the “Curious” form below. It is exactly what a person sees on our web site.

  • What they were promised: an experienced Discovery volunteer will reach out within about two hours, by phone or text (their choice), with no pressure.
  • First name and email are required. Last name is optional. A cell phone number is required when they ask for a call or text, so every request that reaches you has one.
  • “Which program would you like to know more about?” They can choose D1, Relationship Training, or “Not sure yet.” If they aren’t sure, helping them find the right fit is part of your conversation.
  • “Do we have permission to text or call you?” This is required. Their answer tells you how they want to be reached. Always honor it:
    • Yes, call or text is fine — use whichever you prefer.
    • Yes, but text only — do not call.
    • Yes, but call only — do not text.
    • Yes, but please email me — these people are not sent to the CEP Team as requests, so you will not receive them.
  • “Anything you’d like us to know?” Read it before you reach out. It is often the best clue to what they need.
  • They are told we call between 8 am and 8 pm Central, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply STOP to stop texts.

For their security, they will be told your name and phone number, so they will recognize it is a Dallas Discovery Team member.

Module 3 – Getting Ready to Handle Curious Form Inquiries

  1. Give your permission. When you respond to a Curious form request, the applicant is given your name and cell phone number (so that they know it is really us). Your CEP Team Captain will send you a personal link asking for your permission to share your name, cell phone number, and email address. Please open the link and respond. It only takes about a minute. You can withdraw your permission at any time by telling your team Captain or Kent Hafemann.
  2. Set up notifications. An app called “Pushover” sends a notification, with a sound, to your cell phone, iPad, computer, or Apple or Android watch the moment someone asks to hear from us. Nothing is texted, so there are no text-message charges. You will also get an email every time. Pushover is free for the first 30 days, then it requires a one-time purchase of $4.99 plus tax (it is not a subscription).
    1. Download Pushover.
      • On an iPhone, open the App Store and search for Pushover Notifications.
      • On an Android phone, open the Google Play Store and search for Pushover.
      • The app icon looks like this:
      • Install the app.
    2. Create your free account. Open the app, tap Sign Up, and enter your email and a password.
    3. Verify your email address. Pushover sends you an email. Open it and tap the link inside to verify your address. Alerts will not arrive until you complete this.
    4. Allow notifications when the app asks. Without this, you will not hear the alert. (If you tapped “Don’t Allow” by mistake, see the steps below.)
    5. Find your User Key. At the top of the app, you will see a User Key (30 characters). Send the User Key to your CEP Team Captain (text or email). Your Team Captain will enter the key into the CEP portal.
    6. Try it. Ask your Captain to send you a test alert. You should hear it and see it within a few seconds. Tap it. It will open this training page.
    7. If you are not getting alerts, check that your phone is allowed to show them:
      • iPhone: open the Settings app (the gray gear). Tap Notifications, then scroll down the list and tap Pushover. Turn on Allow Notifications. Under “Alerts,” check Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners, and turn on Sounds.
      • Android: open Settings, tap Apps, tap Pushover, tap Notifications, and turn them on.
      • Focus or Do Not Disturb: if your phone is in a Focus mode (the moon icon on an iPhone), alerts may be silenced. Either turn it off during the hours you are on call, or add Pushover to the apps allowed to notify you in that Focus.
      • Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your phone.

Module 4 – The Web Page

When someone completes the Curious form, the portal sends a phone alert and an email to the CEP team asking who wants to handle the request. The alert opens a web page made just for you. If you say “Yes,” the request is yours. (A login and password are not required.) The web page will be used by you to:

  1. Contact the person;
  2. Send a registration link;
  3. Record your progress.

When registration is complete, the portal creates a contact page for that person. Thereafter, the portal automatically sends thank you letters, a questionnaire, and payment requests or reminders. However, there may be times you will want to send the questionnaire, payment reminders, or look at the person’s contact page to make sure it is complete with their photograph and other information. You can do all this through the web page by simply clicking on a few buttons.

Connecting with the Web Page:

  • Tap the phone alert, or the button in the email. Each one opens the page for that request.
  • You can come back at any time by tapping the same alert or email again. Everything you did is still there.
  • The link is yours alone. Please do not share or forward it, because anyone who has it can act as you.

Taking the Curious Form request:

  • On the web page, tap “Yes, I’ll reach out within 2 hours.” The first CEP member to tap gets the request. If someone else already took it, the page tells you, and you will get the next one.
  • The very first time, the page asks you to give your permission first (see Module 3). It takes about a minute, and then you come back and tap yes.

What you see once it is yours:

  • Their name and cell phone number.
  • How they prefer to be contacted. Only the Call and Text buttons that match their choice appear.
  • The training they asked about (D1 or Relationship).
  • What they wrote in the Comments box.

Module 5 – Using the Web Page

The portal wants to know how you’re progressing with the contact. The web page gives you several easy-to-use responses to choose from. The button or box to use is shown next to each action. Items marked coming soon are still being added to the portal.

  1. The contact is not interested; close the request. (Choose Not interested, then tap Save.)
  2. The wrong number or email was provided; close the request. (Choose Wrong number, then tap Save.)
  3. I texted, left a voicemail, or got no answer… I will try again. (Choose Texted, Left a voicemail, or No answer, then tap Save. The request stays open.)
  4. I spoke with the contact and need to follow up. (Choose Spoke with them, then tap Save.)
  5. I sent a registration link and need to follow up. (“Ready to register?” box.)
  6. I sent a questionnaire link and need to follow up. (“Send the questionnaire” box.)
  7. I sent a payment link and need to follow up. (Payment button — coming soon.)
  8. I noticed an incomplete contact page; send a reminder. (Completion button — coming soon.)
  9. I sent a payment follow-up reminder. (Payment follow-up button — coming soon.)
  10. The contact arrived for training; close the request. (Close Request button — coming soon.)
  11. Please transfer this contact to a different CEP member. (Transfer box — coming soon.)
  • Add a short note with your progress: what they hope for, their questions. Don’t forget to tap Save.
  • If you took a request and the portal has not heard from you after two hours, a friendly reminder will be sent.

At the bottom of the Web Page

  • Links to this CEP Team Training guide and copies of the letters we send to contacts, so you can read exactly what they receive.
  • Your CEP Team Captain’s name and number, if you have a question or get stuck.

A few reminders

  • If you cannot make the two-hour window, do not tap yes. Let your CEP Team Captain know if no one else has taken it.
  • What you see on your page is private. Please keep it between you and the team.

Module 6 – Making the connection

Start with a warm, unhurried conversation. Balance energy and enthusiasm with a listening ear.

  1. Reach out the way they asked, using the Call or Text button on your personal page.
  2. Start warmly. For example: “Hi, this is [your name] with Dallas Discovery. You asked to hear more about [training]. Is now a good time?” If it isn’t, offer a time and text to confirm it.
  3. Listen first. Ask what made them curious and what they are hoping for.
  4. Share your own story, briefly and honestly — what you were facing, what you experienced, and what changed.

If you cannot reach them: leave a short, friendly voicemail or text, and try again later that day or the next. Choose “Left a voicemail,” “Texted – waiting for a reply” or “No answer – will try again” to keep the request open.

Answering questions

  • Answer honestly, from your own experience of the training.
  • If you do not know an answer, say so and tell them you will find out. Do not guess about prices, dates or promises.
  • Questions you cannot answer go to your CEP Team Captain.

Respect and privacy

Protect the trust people place in us.

  • Only reach out to people who asked, and only the way they asked.
  • If someone says they are not interested, thank them, log it, and do not follow up again.
  • Keep what people tell you private. Do not share their name, number or story outside the team.

Module 7 – Helping the contact register; sending the questionnaire and payment link

  1. When the contact is ready, tap the “Ready to register?” box on the Web Page.
  2. “Text it from my phone” opens a text to them with their personal link already written. “Email it” sends the same link by email.
  3. The registration form already has their name, email and cell filled in. They choose a training date. For the Relationship Training, their partner’s name and email are also needed. A separate questionnaire will be sent to their partner.
  4. When they submit the form, the contact is enrolled right away, and payment comes later. We email them the details.
  5. Once they have reserved a date, a “Send the questionnaire” box appears on your personal page. Text their private link from your phone, or tap “Email it.” Couples are each sent their own link: emailing sends each person theirs, and texting sends the link for the person you are speaking with. The questionnaire can be closed and reopened from the same link, so they can take their time. The Relationship Training and D1 have different questionnaires.
  6. The Payment button, for sending a payment link, is coming soon.
  7. If the chosen date is full, they are told a team member will reach out to find another date, and the portal is notified.
