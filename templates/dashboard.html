How to use this program

You see only the steps for the areas your login can open. The top bar is your menu.

    {% if session.is_owner %}
  1. Control who has access. Site Access is where you add logins, choose which areas each person can open, and reset passwords.
    2. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_contacts_access %}
  2. Find a person. Open Admin, then Contacts, and search by name or email. Click a name to see the person's page, then Edit contact to open their Contact Information: details, Discovery history, and notes.
    3. {% endif %} {% if can_contacts %}
  3. Add someone new. Open Admin, then Contacts, and use + Add contact. Check first that they are not already there, so no one is entered twice.
  4. Answer requests. Under Admin, Contact requests lists people who asked, on the public site, to be contacted. Follow up, then mark each one handled.
    5. {% endif %} {% if is_lead or has_program_access %}
  5. Schedule a training. Open Programs, find the training, and follow the three red steps: pick the first day, check the last day, then click Schedule this session. Nothing is saved until Step 3.
    6. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_contacts_access %}
  6. See the year at a glance. Calendar shows every scheduled training by month. The public registration page shows the same calendar.
  7. Run a session. Click Open beside a session on Programs to enroll people, assign small groups, and staff the weekend.
  8. Track leaders. Under Admin, Sponsor pipeline shows who is ready to TA or serve as a TA Team Captain, based on how many people they have brought in.
    9. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' %}
  9. Organize data sources. Under Admin, Data Sources records where contacts came from, such as a personal list or an imported database.
    10. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_donations_access %}
  10. Review donations. Donations lists every recorded gift, with totals.
    11. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' %}
  11. Record contracts. A person's contracts are at the bottom of their Contact Information page, once Discovery attendance is Yes. They save themselves as you type.
    12. {% endif %}
  12. Read the mission and the business plan. Mission shows our mission and the Preliminary Business Plan. To send the plan to an interested person, open Admin, then Contacts, and open that person (Leadership only).
  13. Keep your login safe. Use Change password at the top right, and Log out when you finish. You are logged out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
