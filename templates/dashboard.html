How to use this program

You see only the steps for the areas your login can open. The top bar is your menu.

    {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_contacts_access %}
  1. Find a person. Open Contacts and search by name or email. Click a name for the whole record: history, trainings attended, notes, and the contract.
    2. {% endif %} {% if can_contacts %}
  2. Add someone new. Use + Add contact on the Contacts page. Check first that they are not already there, so no one is entered twice.
  3. Answer requests. Contact requests lists people who asked, on the public site, to be contacted. Follow up, then mark each one handled.
    4. {% endif %} {% if is_lead or has_program_access %}
  4. Schedule a training. Open Programs, find the training, and follow the three red steps: pick the first day, check the last day, then click Schedule this session. Nothing is saved until Step 3.
    5. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_contacts_access %}
  5. See the year at a glance. Calendar shows every scheduled training by month. The public registration page shows the same calendar.
  6. Run a session. Click Open beside a session (here, or on Programs) to enroll people, assign small groups, and staff the weekend.
  7. Track leaders. Sponsor pipeline shows who is ready to TA or serve as a TA Team Captain, based on how many people they have brought in.
    8. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' %}
  8. Organize contact lists. Lists records where contacts came from, such as a personal list or an imported database.
    9. {% endif %} {% if session.staff_role == 'Leadership' or has_donations_access %}
  9. Review donations. Donations lists every recorded gift, with totals.
    10. {% endif %} {% if has_contracts_access %}
  10. Read contracts and the business plan. Contracts shows each recorded contract with its history, and the preliminary business plan as people receive it. These pages are view-only.
    11. {% endif %} {% if session.is_owner %}
  11. Control who has access. Site Access is where you add logins, choose which areas each person can open, and reset passwords.
    12. {% endif %}
  12. Keep your login safe. Use Change password at the top right, and Log out when you finish. You are logged out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
