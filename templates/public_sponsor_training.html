Sponsor guide

A short guide for volunteers who sponsor someone.

What a Sponsor does

Registering a person is easy. Follow the instructions below, and if needed an experienced volunteer will call you to provide assistance.

  1. Click the “Sponsor a Friend” button below and complete the form for your friend.
  2. Note that, for transparency, we ask your permission to send your contact information to your friend.
  3. When the form is complete, you will receive an email asking you to fill in some basic information about yourself (this helps relieve the administrative burden). Then you will receive a private web page for your friend that walks you through the registration process.

Be a good sponsor

  • Only sponsor someone you have spoken with who would welcome hearing from us.
  • Listen first. Share your own story briefly and honestly. No pressure.
  • If you do not know an answer, say “I’ll find out.” Do not guess about prices, dates or promises.
  • Keep what people tell you private, and keep your page link to yourself.

Thanks so much. We greatly appreciate your help.
