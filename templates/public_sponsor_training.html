Sponsor guide

A short guide for volunteers who sponsor someone.

What a Sponsor does

A Sponsor is a volunteer who invites one person to Discovery! and stays with them from their first question all the way into the training room. You are their personal contact, and you help them register, pay, and arrive ready.

How it works

  1. Fill out the short Sponsor form on the website with your own details and the name, email and cell of the person you would like to sponsor, and give your permission to share your contact information with them.
  2. We email you a letter. It asks you to complete your own long form, including a photo, and explains the private web page you are about to receive.
  3. Your private page opens as soon as your long form is complete. It is made for that one person. Reach out with the Call or Text buttons, then log how it went.
  4. When they are ready, tap a button to send their registration link. After they reserve, send the questionnaire link, the payment request and the long form link. Your page shows a checklist that fills itself in, and a “Your next step” box that always tells you what to do now.
  5. If payment is late, we nudge you every 7 days. A reminder is your call. Once payment arrives, we send a thank-you automatically, and “What to bring” one week before the training.

Be a good sponsor

  • Only sponsor someone you have spoken with who would welcome hearing from us.
  • Listen first. Share your own story briefly and honestly. No pressure.
  • If you do not know an answer, say “I’ll find out.” Do not guess about prices, dates or promises.
  • Keep what people tell you private, and keep your page link to yourself.
