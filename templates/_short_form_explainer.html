What it is. The short form is the quick form on the website. It takes about a minute and asks for very little. There are two versions:

  • The “I'm curious” form (front page, program pages and the Reserve page): name, email, cell number, which program, whether they would like a call or a text, and permission to call and text them. It starts the Guide process.
  • The Discovery! form for past volunteers and friends of the program: name, email, phone, their Discovery! role, and an optional message.

Discovery! history. On the Discovery! form, the person picks their past role (Facilitator, TA, Trainee and others, or “Interested Party” if they have none) and the month and year, and can add more than one. The “I'm curious” form does not ask about history.

What it does. It creates the person in Contacts as an Interested Party, or matches them to a record we already have, and it can send the welcome letter. People who tick “Volunteer” on the Discovery! form are emailed the long form straight away.

How it ties to the long form. The short form creates the person's record and the long form adds to that same record through a private link, so nothing is typed twice.
