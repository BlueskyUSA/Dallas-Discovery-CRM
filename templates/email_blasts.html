Caution: purchased or third-party databases

Do not buy a database, and do not email one, until we have researched every requirement through Claude.

  • Brevo (our email service) generally does not allow sending to purchased or rented lists, and an account can be suspended for it. That would stop the portal’s other emails too.
  • U.S. law (CAN-SPAM) also requires a working unsubscribe link and a physical mailing address in commercial email, and other laws may apply.
  • For a database someone lets us download, keep a record of their permission to email it.
  • Keep each source on its own list, so it can be found and removed later.
