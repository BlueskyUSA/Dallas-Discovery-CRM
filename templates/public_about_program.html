About {{ public_brand }}

{{ public_brand }} was founded by Coach Kent Hafemann, his wife Pamela Hafemann (MA, LPC, LCDC), and Coach Max Thompson with a vision to give people a guided, safe, and honest space to look at their lives — where they've been, where they are, and where they're headed — and to help couples build stronger, healthier long term-relationships. Our trainings are experiential, small-group based, and led by a seasoned team of trained facilitators and assistants who've walked the path themselves.

Our personal-growth seminars move through three stages — The Beginning, Awakening, and Vision — each a three-day experience that builds on the one before and is taken in sequence. The Art of Us is a fun, challenging three-day seminar for couples (or committed relationships) and stands on its own, open to anyone. There is no prerequisite for The Art of Us or The Beginning, though many people who attend The Art of Us want additional opportunities to explore the mastery of successful relationships.
