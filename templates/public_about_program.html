About {{ program_name }}

{{ program_name }} was founded by Coach Kent Hafemann, his wife Pamela Hafemann (MA, LPC, LCDC), and Coach Max Thompson with a vision to give people a guided, safe, and honest space to look at their lives — where they've been, where they are, and where they're headed — and to help couples build stronger, healthier long term-relationships. Our trainings are experiential, small-group based, and led by a seasoned team of trained facilitators and assistants who've walked the path themselves.

Our monthly core life training moves through three stages — D1, D2, & D3 — taken in sequence over three months. Each training builds on the prior month and cannot be taken out of sequence. So, it is recommended, but not required, that all three trainings be taken in consecutive months. However, for couples (or committed relationships), the Relationship Training is a practical and wisdom filled coaching weekend as a standalone experience open to anyone. There is no prerequisite to attend D1, D2 & D3, although most people who attend the relationship training want additional opportunities to explore the mastery of successful relationships.
