Team accounts

Leadership accounts help run the CRM but only see confidential areas (Contacts, Donations, Contracts, program materials) once the Owner authorizes them. Accounting & Legal logins (accountant, bookkeeper, attorney) are view-only and can only be given Accounting, Donations, Contracts, or Contacts. Only the Owner creates or changes Accounting & Legal logins, sets who can open which areas, and resets passwords. Deactivating an account keeps its history but blocks it from logging in.
