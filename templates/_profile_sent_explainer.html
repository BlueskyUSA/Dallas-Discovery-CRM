How the Profile gets sent (the “Complete your profile” page, also called the long form)

Everyone has one private Profile link. The Profile is complete when Basic info and Personal info are filled in. Discovery history is welcome but not required. The Hub does not need to track whether it was sent; each contact’s page shows what has been completed.

Sent automatically

  • Website form, Volunteer box checked: the “Profile follow-up” email (below) goes to the person.
  • Sponsor a Friend form: the “Sponsor welcome (complete your profile)” email (under Volunteers → Sponsors).
  • Helper sign-up: the Volunteer welcome email includes their Profile link (under Other Volunteers).

Sent by a person

  • A Guide: “Email it” under Next step sends “Complete your profile” (under Volunteers → Guides), or the Guide sends it by text.
  • You, from a contact’s page: the Email request button (the wording is “Profile update request”, below), or Text request, or copy the private link.
  • The session team: on a session page, the button to email team members who have not answered the food and physical needs questions (“Food and physical needs request”, below) also points to the Profile.

Open link

  • The general “Complete your profile” link on the public site, for posting or a mass email. Whoever uses it creates their own contact record.

To see who still needs to finish, open Contacts and use the filter “Profile incomplete”, “Basic info incomplete” or “Personal info incomplete”.
