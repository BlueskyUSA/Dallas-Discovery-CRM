Our Story, Our Mission

Most of us have heard the expression “don’t waste the pain.” The Co-founders of Discovery, Pamela and Kent, were both raised in broken homes. In our youth, our natural parents were married sixteen times to different people, and we both moved from place to place many times. We suffered significant trauma, abandonment, and upheaval. The impact of these life circumstances was significant beyond our young imagination.

I was 24 and Pamela was 23 when we started discussing the idea of marriage. We both were scared spitless, since by all measures of life calculus, the odds of living successfully were minimal at best. We were in college, broke, and clueless about what it takes to remain in a marriage and be happy. But we had stars in our eyes, lots of hope, and a mutual commitment to live life differently. We were married on March 18, 1978.

Having been married about 12 years, our marriage was in trouble. We had three children and lots of responsibility. I was a VP in a large real estate investment firm and very active as a leader in our church. Pamela was mostly a stay-at-home mom raising our children. But she too was a leader in our church, and to help with the bills, she took a part-time sales job working for a lighting company. We both were college graduates, and most who knew us considered us to be smart, successful, and living the dream. But our marriage was falling apart at the seams, and we couldn’t figure out why.

We engaged a counselor and sought help from the church and our friends. Yet despite our best efforts, we could not figure out why our marriage was failing until in 1996, we went through a powerful life training program very similar to Discovery, and it changed our lives forever. The tools we were given in this training helped us uncover and manage the crippling emotional luggage we carried around. Through this experience we recognized most human beings have similar wounds and baggage that, if left un-examined, keep us mired in brokenness. Not long after we completed this training we resolved to be a force for healing and wholeness in our world. Sage wisdom is hard earned and takes many years to culminate. {{ public_brand }} offers you a powerful, safe place to search out knowledge, to find freedom from what bogs you down, and invite joy into your life.
