Site Access Components

How access to the site and the programs is divided. Access works in two layers: who you are (your role) and which areas you have been given. The people shown are live.

1. Who: the four kinds of login

Owner

Automatically has every area. The only one who can give someone everything and manage Accounting & Legal logins.

Leadership

Runs the CRM day to day. Sees confidential areas only once specifically given them, unless the Owner grants everything.

Team (Volunteer Team)

Volunteers. Sees only the areas they have been given.

Accounting & Legal

The accountant, bookkeeper and attorney. View only. Can only be given Accounting, Donations, Contracts and Contacts.
