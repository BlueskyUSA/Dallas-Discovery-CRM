A journey of personal growth, human connection, and curiosity

Heal. Discover. Strengthen. Transform.

Discovery! offers four immersive, three-day experiential seminars designed to help you strengthen your relationships, grow in self-awareness, and live with greater confidence, clarity, and purpose.

Three of these programs form a progressive journey—understanding your past, becoming more aware of your present, and envisioning your future.

The Art of Us is a standalone seminar for couples. It is a fun, interactive, and challenging experience that invites couples into deeper honesty, intimacy, and more effective communication in their marriage or committed relationship.

Throughout Discovery!, you’ll have opportunities to explore the beliefs and patterns that shape your life, discover new perspectives, and develop practical tools for creating a more intentional, connected, and fulfilling life.
