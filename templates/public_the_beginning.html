The Beginning

What if there’s more to your story than the version you’ve been telling yourself?

Understand your past. Discover new possibilities.

Every story has a beginning.

Along the way, we develop beliefs about who we are, what we can accomplish, what we deserve, and how the world works.

Some of these beliefs encourage us; others may quietly influence our decisions and relationships in ways we don’t fully recognize.

Sometimes the most powerful discoveries happen when we recognize a little of ourselves in someone else’s story.

The Beginning is an immersive, three-day experiential seminar that invites you to explore those early influences with curiosity and compassion. The goal is not to remain in the past, but to better understand it, and discover possibilities for how you live today.

Where this fits

The first step of the journey.

The Beginning is the first step in the three-part personal-growth sequence. It has no prior Discovery! seminar requirement. Completing it prepares you to participate in Awakening, followed by Vision.

Tentative schedule: Planned for April, with additional sessions alongside Awakening in May and alongside Awakening and Vision in June. Exact dates and registration details will be announced.
